# Sources and media notes

## Principal session source

**StandWithUs UK, Northern Arrows presentation**, supplied as `Northern Arrows Presentation(1).pdf`, 78 pages.

* Pages 8 to 11: French mandate, early demographics and political institutions.
* Pages 14 to 16: Political crisis in 1958, US intervention and Beirut's later relative stability.
* Pages 18 to 29: PLO movements and the 1975 civil war.

The introductory demographic bars use the slide's approximate historic and more recent figures. They are **illustrative and not established census results**. The presentation itself refers to religious communities as an ethnic composition. In this edition, the chart labels are corrected to describe religious communities rather than ethnicities.

**Census limitation:** Lebanon has not held an official national population census since 1932. Recent estimates can change depending on whether they cover Lebanese citizens, residents, displaced people, or particular definitions of religious communities.

## Image correction

The photograph previously used for the 1958 chapter showed Egyptian President Gamal Abdel Nasser. It has been replaced with the photograph of military personnel behind a sandbag position in the **US Intervention** section on slide 14 of the supplied presentation. It should not be presented as a photograph of Camille Chamoun.
