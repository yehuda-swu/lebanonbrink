# Historical sources and media credits

## Presentation

**StandWithUs UK, Northern Arrows** (user supplied 78 page PDF):

* Pages 8 to 11: French mandate, religious community estimates and political structure.
* Pages 14 to 16: The 1958 crisis, US intervention and subsequent years of stability.
* Pages 18 to 29: The PLO's influence, growing unrest and the outbreak of the Lebanese Civil War.

The demographic illustrations retain the approximate proportions found in the presentation: 1920 about 53 per cent Christian, 23 per cent Sunni Muslim, 19 per cent Shia Muslim, and 5 per cent Druze; a more recent comparison in the presentation gives 32, 32, 31 and 5 per cent respectively. These figures are **not official or directly comparable census values**. Lebanon's last official national census took place in 1932. Population estimates depend on the methodology, geographical reach, period and definitions employed.
