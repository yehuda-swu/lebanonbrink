## Editorial conventions

* The 1943 agreement is called the National Pact. Full French military withdrawal occurred in 1946.
* The PLO's strength in Lebanon increased after the conflicts in Jordan in 1970 and 1971. The 1969 Cairo Agreement preceded that expansion.
* The Lebanese Civil War is described as extending from 1975 to 1990.
* All gameplay options, dialogue paraphrases, and meter impacts are hypothetical and do not purport to reconstruct historical cabinet decisions.
